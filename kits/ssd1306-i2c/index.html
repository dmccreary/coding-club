    Tiny SSD1306 I2C OLED Clock¶

    An ideal starter clock using a small 1" OLED with a simple four-wire I2C connection.

    Source book: Clocks and Watches
    Kit page: https://dmccreary.github.io/clocks-and-watches/kits/ssd1306-i2c/
    Printable one-pager: ssd1306-i2c-one-pager.html

    Features¶

    • Lab: Adjusting the Clock Digit Layout
    • Lab: Integrating the Local Time
    • Lab: Prompt for the SSD1306 I2C Clock
    • Lab: Manually Setting Time on Power Up

    Benefits¶

    • Free, open lessons: the Clocks and Watches textbook has the kit page, wiring, and MicroPython code.
    • Ready-made curriculum: 4 additional lab pages walk students through the build step by step.
    • Instant feedback: students see their code's results on lights or a display.
    • Budget: about $14 (0.96" OLED about $4.40, Pico W $5, breadboard $2, wires $1, USB cable $2).

    Parts¶

    • Raspberry Pi Pico ($4) — Microcontroller
    • SSD1306 128×64 OLED — Display
    • 830-Point Breadboard ($1.10) — Breadboard
    • Micro-USB Data Cable ($2.50) — Power and programming cable
