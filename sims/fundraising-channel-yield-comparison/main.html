Typical Yield vs. Volunteer Hours by Fundraising Channel

What This Shows

In-kind donation yields the most value for the fewest hours: 750 dollars of value for about 2 hours. Fundraising events are the opposite - 420 dollars for 15 hours.
Read the two bars together, not separately. The highest-yield channel is not the best choice if the club does not have the hours. A club with 5 volunteer hours a month should be looking at sponsorship and in-kind donations; the event only makes sense when the club has people who want to run one.
