    List of MicroSims for Coding Club¶

    Interactive Micro Simulations to help students learn coding club fundamentals.

    • Access Barrier to Fix Map

      Five barriers families face, seven club practices that address them -- and the one barrier three fixes converge on.

    • AI Agent Loop Explorer

      Input, draft, human review, send -- stepped through with the real text, and one confidently wrong detail in every draft.

    • AI Tool Selection Radar

      Three vendors on four criteria at once -- and a ranking that changes depending on which criterion your club puts first.

    • Anatomy of a Challenge Card

      Six required parts of a challenge card, and what each one is for.

    • Anatomy of a Club Charter

      A club charter is one document made of five distinct sections. See what each section governs and how they differ.

    • Annotated Feedback Form

      Three question types on one short form, and what each one buys and costs.

    • Anonymized Attendance Chart

      Read a semester's attendance trend -- including the break-week dip and the year-over-year comparison -- from data holding no names at all.

    • Attendance and Enrollment Trend Explorer

      Four semesters of enrollment, attendance and retention on one chart -- and the semester where they disagree.

    • Automated Communication Pipeline

      One trigger, two very different-feeling messages: see what decides whether a rule sends a text or queues a newsletter item.

    • Bias and Privacy Risk Sorter

      Eight scenarios, four bins, and one distinction that is easy to state and hard to apply.

    • Blinking LED Breadboard Circuit

      A working blink circuit on a real breadboard layout -- with a button that removes one jumper wire and kills it.

    • Board Governance Cycle

      The routine seat-rotation cycle a board runs on, and the separate trigger a founder's departure fires.

    • Bookmark Folder Structure

      Decide where a new bookmark belongs: the shared class folder, a student's personal folder, or neither.

    • Breadboard Tie Point Explorer

      Click any hole on a half-size breadboard and every hole electrically tied to it lights up.

    • Circuit Symbol Reader

      Five schematic symbols on flashcards, with a quiz that scores a full pass.

    • Circuit Troubleshooting Workflow

      Work the four checks in the order that eliminates the cheapest and most likely faults first.

    • Club Calendar Setup Workflow

      The five one-time steps that set up a shared club calendar correctly, in the order that avoids rework.

    • Club Financial Operations Cycle

      Follow one dollar from the moment it is spent or given through to the annual budget review.

    • Club Governance Map

      Who reports to whom, who provides outside oversight, and where a mentor actually sits in the structure.

    • Club Growth Path

      Four growth stages on one timeline, each with the single condition that has to be true before a club moves on, plus a marker to bookmark where you actually are.

    • Club Health Metrics Dashboard

      Four health metrics across three years, and the one line moving the wrong way while the others celebrate.

    • Club Inventory Lifecycle Workflow

      One tagged item's whole life: sticker, shelf, checkout, return, audit, and the branch into the lost item policy.

    • Coding Club Budget Builder

      A first-year budget built line by line, split into what recurs and what does not.

    • Coding Club Ecosystem Map

      Place a coding club precisely among six neighbouring educational contexts, and see what makes it distinct from each one.

    • Coding Club Room Layout

      A club room floor plan where every placement is clickable -- and a draggable mentor marker that shows what facing the monitors inward actually buys you.

    • Collision Avoidance Control Loop

      Sense, decide, act -- with a polling interval you can widen until the robot drives into a wall it thinks is 40 cm away.

    • Color Wheel Hue Explorer

      Drag a marker round a 0-255 color wheel and see the exact RGB triple the wheel() function would return.

    • Data Backup Routine Timeline

      A semester of backups in five checkpoints, only two of which need a person -- and the one that matters is the restore test, not the backup.

    • Data Retention Schedule by Type

      Four kinds of club data, four different clocks -- and what event starts each one.

    • Device End-of-Life Decision Workflow

      Route a retiring laptop to donation or to responsible disposal, and see how one club's retirement becomes another's intake.

    • Device Lifecycle Workflow

      Follow one club laptop from imaging through circulation to the three-way condition check that decides what happens next.

    • Differential Drive Chassis Explorer

      Two wheel speeds, and every path a two-wheel robot can take -- named in words before the robot moves.

    • Digital Signal HIGH/LOW Chart

      A digital pin's voltage over time: two values, vertical transitions, nothing in between.

    • Electrical Safety Rules Poster

      Four hazard families, three rules each -- the panel you point at depends on what is on the table.

    • Email List Segments

      Four club mailing segments, drawn so the overlaps that exist are visible and the one that must not is visibly absent.

    • Equity Audit Checklist

      Ten items, five categories, and a callout that always names one thing to do rather than a score to feel bad about.

    • Feedback Loop Cycle

      The four stages that keep survey responses from disappearing into an unread inbox -- and why the last one is the load-bearing stage.

    • Four Coding Club Scheduling Models

      Alternating Saturday, weekday after-school, semester-based, drop-in -- and what each one costs you.

    • Frequency Spectrum Display Explorer

      Three sounds, three unmistakable bar shapes -- and the habit of naming the shape before naming the sound.

    • From Draft to Delivered - AI Content Pipeline

      Three AI drafting agents, three different jobs, one review gate every draft must pass.

    • From Observation to Customized Lesson

      Where the mentor's own observation enters an AI customization loop, and where human review still applies.

    • From Scattered Knowledge to a Club Playbook

      Decide whether a piece of know-how becomes a written procedure or has to be transferred person to person.

    • Frustration Escalation Response Flow

      Match the tool to the trigger: a hard moment, a pattern across weeks, or a task that just failed.

    • Funding Partnership Fit Map

      Four funding needs, four partnership approaches, and which one solves more than one need.

    • Fundraising Channel Yield Comparison

      Five fundraising channels compared on the two axes that actually decide the choice: dollars raised and volunteer hours spent.

    • Generic vs AI Challenge Card

      The same repeat-block lesson, twice -- and the three fields customisation is allowed to touch.

    • Grant Budget Laptop Calculator

      Grant, unit price, student count -- and the leftover nobody plans for.

    • Grant Writing and Tracking Workflow

      From spotting an opportunity to logging the outcome -- and why every branch ends at the tracking spreadsheet.

    • Growth Mindset Coaching Response Flow

      Three kinds of student moment, three different right responses -- and one shared goal.

    • Guardian Consent Collection Workflow

      Trace where 'collection begins' sits relative to 'consent obtained' -- and why the order is not negotiable.

    • Gyroscope Orientation Data Explorer

      Tip a sensor board three ways and watch exactly one of the three readings move each time.

    • H-Bridge Direction and Speed Control

      Two logic inputs pick a diagonal of switches; a PWM slider sets the speed. The microcontroller never carries motor current.

    • Handling a Conflict or Complaint

      The step-by-step path a coding club follows from a reported concern to a documented outcome, including when to escalate.

    • Human-in-the-Loop Review Workflow

      Trace an AI draft through a guardrail check, a human quality check, and the single moment a person actually sends it.

    • Institutional Funding Partnership Map

      Four ongoing funding needs, four institutional partners, and the five connections between them. Two nodes carry more than one line, and those are the ones worth studying.

    • Interest Survey Results

      47 survey responses grouped by age range and interest level -- enough to decide whether a club is worth starting, and for whom.

    • Intrinsic vs Extrinsic Motivation Map

      Three columns, and the middle one is the only place a reward belongs.

    • Kit Lifecycle Workflow

      Follow one kit from a gap on the shelf through purchase, prep, circulation, and the three-way decision that decides its fate.

    • Kits Ready to Go Further

      Two step-up kits, what each one does, and what a mentor would actually see running.

    • Layers of Youth Digital Safety

      Five layers, bottom to top, and who manages each one.

    • Leaderboard vs Collaborative Challenge

      Three competition formats on a risk gradient, and the one that hurts the students who need encouragement most.

    • Learning Graph Viewer

      Explore the full concept dependency graph for the course. Search for a concept, filter by category, and see how many nodes and edges are visible as you narrow the view.

    • LED Anatomy

      Four parts of an LED, and the two independent ways to tell which leg is which.

    • LED Matrix Pattern Designer

      Draw on an 8x8 grid and watch the eight bytes that would produce it appear beside the picture.

    • Levels of Intelligent Textbook Sophistication

      Six rungs from a static PDF to an adaptive AI tutor, with a marker showing where this book actually sits.

    • Meet the Club's AI Agent Roster

      Six AI agent types around one umbrella idea -- click any to see the club task it handles and a real example.

    • Meet the Specialized Agent Roster

      Five agents for the occasional jobs -- grants, social media, website copy, translation, voice -- and who reviews each one's output.

    • Membership Fee Model Comparison

      Flat fee against sliding scale on the same families -- with the reach that a revenue bar alone does not show.

    • Mentor Development Pipeline

      One candidate's whole path: interview, trial, training, tiers, and the pipeline that produces the club's next leader.

    • Mentor Matching and Schedule Optimizer

      Five mentors, five students, seven scored pairings, and three candidate slots -- only one of which passes every constraint.

    • Mentor Recruiting Channel Effectiveness

      Five recruiting channels compared on both volume and quality, because the channel that produces the most candidates produces the fewest mentors.

    • Mentor Recruiting Influence Graph

      Two outreach channels the club already uses, four it does not, and the question of which single new connection would reach the most new people.

    • Mentor-Gated Registration Flow

      See how registering mentors first turns a 3:1 ratio into a capacity limit the software enforces, instead of a rule mentors have to police.

    • Message Channel Decision Tree

      Two questions decide whether a message goes to the group chat, to email, or either.

    • MicroPython Syntax Checker

      Six snippets, two classic mistakes, and indentation drawn as visible guides so an off-by-one space is something you can see.

    • Motor Type Comparison Explorer

      DC, servo, stepper -- three icons that move the way their motors do, matched against six real project requirements.

    • Moving Rainbow Wiring Diagram

      Three wires between a Pico and a NeoPixel strip, with a quiz that hides the labels.

    • Multimeter Usage Simulator

      Drag two probes onto a real circuit in three meter modes -- and find a short the meter can see but your eyes cannot.

    • NeoPixel Loop Pixel Stepper

      Step a for loop over a 30-pixel strip one iteration at a time, and watch an off-by-one error leave the first pixel dark.

    • Normal Circuit vs. Short Circuit

      The same battery, twice: once through a resistor at 27 mA, once through bare wire at about 6 A.

    • Notification Lead Time Timeline

      Two reminders doing two different jobs: one buys planning time, one prevents forgetting, and a single reminder can only do one of them.

    • Ohm's Law Current Calculator

      Set a supply voltage and a series resistor, and read the LED current before you power a real circuit.

    • OLED Coordinate System

      Find any (x, y) pixel on a 128x64 OLED display and see the MicroPython command that lights it.

    • Oversight Board Org Chart

      Five board seats, what each one does, and how a board sits above day-to-day sessions without running them.

    • Peripheral and Storage Management Layers

      Five categories covering every accessory question a club actually gets.

    • Photoresistor Voltage Divider Explorer

      Light level in, resistance and divider voltage out -- with a curve showing why the fixed resistor choice matters.

    • Pico REPL vs Script Workflow

      Three ways to run code on a Pico, separated by one question: what is still running after you pull the cable out?

    • PIR Detection Zone Map

      Why a PIR sensor sees someone walking across a room and misses someone standing still in front of it.

    • Pivot Table Builder

      Twenty raw sign-in rows, two dropdowns, and a grand total that never changes.

    • Platform Management Workflow

      See where a new laptop's operating system sends it, and what managing each of the three platforms actually involves.

    • Potentiometer Analog Input Explorer

      Turn a knob and watch an analog reading sweep smoothly through 1024 values, then see what a digital pin would have made of the same knob.

    • Progress Dashboard Explorer

      Filter a badge-completion dashboard by cohort and watch the bottleneck move -- with initials only, no names anywhere.

    • Promotional Materials Recruiting Rubric

      Five criteria, ten points, three sample flyers -- and the lowest row matters more than the total.

    • Prompt Refinement Step-Through

      Add audience, tone, length, and the must-include detail one at a time, and watch the edits left drop to zero.

    • Push Button Digital Input Circuit

      Press a button, watch the input pin flip to HIGH -- then remove the pull-down resistor and watch LOW stop being reliable.

    • PWM Analog Output Brightness Explorer

      See how a pin that can only be fully on or fully off produces every brightness in between.

    • Raspberry Pi Pico Pinout Explorer

      Click any pin on the Pico board to see what it does, what voltage it runs at, and the MicroPython line that uses it.

    • Resistor Color Code Calculator

      Set the four colored bands on a resistor and read off its value, so a mentor can check a part before it goes into a circuit.

    • Robot Emotion Display Control Panel

      One distance reading driving a face and a buzzer at the same time -- plus the hysteresis band that stops both flickering.

    • Robot Kit Assembly Sequence

      Six build steps to drag into order, each with the mechanical reason it has to come when it does.

    • Saving Student Data Decision Tool

      Two questions, in order, applied to any field somebody wants to collect.

    • Seasonal LED Pattern Gallery

      Four strips running the same algorithm, and the two or three numbers that make each one a different season.

    • Sensor Type Picker

      Eight project scenarios, six sensors, and one question that settles every one of them.

    • Servo Angle and Pulse Width Explorer

      Set a pulse width in microseconds and watch the servo arm swing to the angle it encodes.

    • Session Room Safety and Logistics Map

      Eight things every session room needs, and where each one goes.

    • Seven Segment Display Explorer

      Choose a digit and see exactly which of the seven segments switch on to draw it.

    • Should You Start This Club?

      Walk the four feasibility gates between an encouraging survey result and a real first meeting date.

    • Showcase Wall vs Peer Recognition Activity

      A standing display of finished work, or a five-minute circle at the end of every session -- they solve different problems.

    • Soldering Safety Workflow

      Rehearse the before, during, and after habits of soldering in the order a mentor actually performs them.

    • Sound Threshold Detector

      Three rooms, one clap, and a threshold line you can drag until the detector works -- or stops working.

    • Spreadsheet Template Zones

      Three zones in a tracking spreadsheet, and why the order of the columns is not arbitrary.

    • Static Electricity Precaution Checklist

      One room, five things -- two of them safe to touch, three of them ways to build a charge.

    • Stepper Motor Step Sequence

      Click through the four-coil energizing cycle and watch the rotation total climb 1.8 degrees at a time.

    • Student Data Privacy Principles

      Four practices, four specific risks. Click any one to see exactly which failure it prevents.

    • Student Growth to Portfolio Network

      Five stages from a mentor's in-session note to a family's read-only view -- and what changes at each step.

    • Student Progress Tracking Cycle

      The four-stage habit that keeps a student's record current, and lets a different mentor pick up where the last one left off.

    • Survey Themes and Sentiment Dashboard

      Free-text survey answers grouped into themes and scored for tone -- revealing a pattern an average rating would bury.

    • Temperature Sensor Reading Explorer

      A TMP36 reports volts, not degrees -- watch the conversion formula fill in with live numbers.

    • The AI Agent Rollout Pipeline

      Five stages between choosing a tool and using it club-wide -- and what breaks if you skip each one.

    • The Club Improvement Cycle

      Step one session's observation all the way around the club improvement loop, from raw note to a change in how the club runs.

    • The Event Retrospective Process

      Follow one session from its final minute to a change in the next session, through the debrief, the survey, and the log.

    • The Five Computational Thinking Skills

      Decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction, algorithm design, debugging -- with a club example for each.

    • The Physical Computing Loop

      Input, process, output -- stepped one stage at a time, with the actual data each stage is holding.

    • The Tangible Reward Ladder

      Six recognition tools on six rungs, ordered by the size of the thing they mark.

    • Three Curriculum Tracks

      Beginner, intermediate, advanced -- what each track actually contains, and where the hard transition sits.

    • Three Kinds of Mentors

      Volunteer, professional, peer -- three mentor types whose strengths do not overlap.

    • Ultrasonic Ranging Explorer

      Send a pulse at a wall, watch the echo come back, and see how a duration in microseconds becomes a distance in centimetres.

    • USB Connector Comparison

      Two plug shapes, six callouts, and a flip control that shows exactly why one of them fits either way up.

    • Where a Coding-Help Request Goes

      Three coding-help agents, three different questions they answer -- and the mentor who stays in the room for all of them.
