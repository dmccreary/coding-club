# AI Racing League Story

Please create a new story called "AI Racing League".

Here are the main characters.  Please make them consistent across the entire story.

 The main character is about "Charlie" a gray-haired but fun-loving retired engineer who wears very colorful Hawaiian shirts and looking for meaning in life after a long but successful engineering career.
 
 Charlie is 66 year old white male, slightly balding, and is 5"10" tall.  He lives in a quiet suburban neighborhood with his wife "Joan".  Joan has dark brown hair with a few gray streaks.
 
Linda is the principal at the local high-school.  She is a professionally dressed black woman with an optimistic attitude.

 Panel 1: The Retirement Party
 Scene one is Charlies' retirement party at a high-tech AI-centric electrical engineering company office.  In this panel Charlies's co-workers and friends are all surrounding Charlie and congratulating him on a successful career.
 
 Panel 2: "Title: What are Your Post Retirement Plans?"
 At the retirement party one person asks: "Charlies, What are your plans after retirement".
 Charlie answers "I have no idea.  I am still trying to figure that out."

Panel 3: "Title: Get a Hobby"
 Charlie is at home driving his wife Joan crazy by putting labels on every bin in the shop/garage.
 Joan wife tells him "Charlie, you needs to get a hobby". 
 
Panel 4: The "Donkey Car" Video that Sparked an Idea
The next day Charlie is online and he sees a video about the "Donkey Car" - a way to mount a Raspberry Pi on top of an 1/16th scale RC car and add a camera.  Then you train it to drive around a track using machine learning.  This gives Charlie an idea!  He can volunteer at a local high-school.


Panel 5: The Meeting with the Principal

Charlie contacts a local inner city high school and says he wants to start a project to teach AI to students in the school with a focus on underserved youth and girls.  When he meets the principal Linda she is at first unsure of Charlie.  


Panel 6: The Donkey Car Demo
Will he follow through?  But Charlie is persistent and brings by a Donkey Car for Linda and the staff to see.  There is a lot of excitement and Linda sees that the idea has some merit.  Linda wants her school to be more AI literate and she is getting convinced that this might be a fun way to get started.

Panel 7: The Grant Letter
One day Charlie and Joan are at home an Joan brings in a letter from the school.
When they open it they see they gets a grant of $2,000, which can buys a GPU and three car kits to starts the club. 

Panel 8: The Event Sign Up Day
At the start of the school year the school has a sign up day clubs and extra curricular events.
Unfortunately only a three students sign up at first because their friends were already doing a traditional "gears and motors RC event" taught by the shop teachers

Panel 9: Initial Challenge: Dead Batteries
Charlie and the team had many challenges.  For example the batteries that
came with the car ran out in 7 minutes of driving.  This was not enough time to gather the 10,000
images to train the AI mode.  Charlie was disappointed that some of the first events did not go very well.  Finally Charlie found a replacement battery that lasted 30 minutes.

Panel 10: Challenge #2 Free Software is Not Well Documented
The Donkey car software was not very mature.  It assumed you knew a lot about Python and machine learning.  It came with little documentation
and didn't run consistently.  The calibration process was slow and error prone.  - poor documentation of the open source Donkey Car software.
But this turned out to be a blessing.  The team learned that problem solving skills were
sometimes as important as learning Python or Machine Learning

Panel 11: Race Day Demo Event
After working the bugs out of the car and getting stable software the club schedule a big
Race Day Demo event. The club took over the high-school gym and has races to see what car could drive 10 times around the track without any manual assistance.  

Panel 12: Success Comes Slowly
Eventually the AI Racing League club becomes very popular and the school challenges other schools.
Scene: A high-school gym with a large Donkey Car track laid out on the floor.  The track is black and has two lanes with a dashed yellow line down the middle of the track.

Panel 13: Charlie at Home
The final scene is Charlie at home in his shop soldering wires on a Donkey Car.  His wife Joan is in the background smiling.  Charlie is finally happy in retirement.  He has found meaning and purpose.
