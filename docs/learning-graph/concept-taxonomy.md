# Concept Taxonomy

This taxonomy organizes the Coding Club learning graph's concepts into 15 categories.

## Coding Club Foundations (FOUND)

The nature and history of coding clubs, their evolution, and the role of AI in modern club operations and intelligent textbooks.

- **TaxonomyID:** FOUND
- **Concept count:** 36 (5.5% of total)

## Governance And Charter (GOV)

Club charters, values, policies, safety rules, and the roles and responsibilities that keep a club legally and ethically sound.

- **TaxonomyID:** GOV
- **Concept count:** 32 (4.9% of total)

## Launching And Session Design (LAUNCH)

Gauging interest, holding a first meeting, promoting the club, growing it over time, and designing the physical classroom and session flow.

- **TaxonomyID:** LAUNCH
- **Concept count:** 70 (10.7% of total)

## Mentors Students And Ratios (PEOPLE)

Mentors, students, the 3:1 ratio, recruiting and retaining both groups, and the day-to-day relationship between them.

- **TaxonomyID:** PEOPLE
- **Concept count:** 42 (6.4% of total)

## Scheduling Registration And Events (SCHED)

Choosing meeting times, registration systems, waiting lists, post-event surveys, and running the full event lifecycle.

- **TaxonomyID:** SCHED
- **Concept count:** 52 (7.9% of total)

## Computational Thinking And Curriculum (CT)

Computational thinking skills and the coding curriculum itself: Scratch, Python, Turtle Graphics, and how lessons are sequenced.

- **TaxonomyID:** CT
- **Concept count:** 50 (7.6% of total)

## Physical Computing And Electronics (PHYS)

The electronics fundamentals behind physical computing: circuits, sensors, displays, motors, and robot components.

- **TaxonomyID:** PHYS
- **Concept count:** 55 (8.4% of total)

## Project Kits And Hardware (KITS)

The specific project kits used in sessions -- Moving Rainbow, robot, display, sensor, sound, and IoT kits -- and how they are managed.

- **TaxonomyID:** KITS
- **Concept count:** 60 (9.1% of total)

## Devices And IT Management (TECH)

Purchasing, configuring, and maintaining the laptops and Chromebooks students use, plus accounts, network, and device lifecycle.

- **TaxonomyID:** TECH
- **Concept count:** 44 (6.7% of total)

## Data Tracking And Communication (DATA)

Tracking student progress, managing communication tools, and handling club data responsibly.

- **TaxonomyID:** DATA
- **Concept count:** 42 (6.4% of total)

## Motivation And Recognition (MOTIV)

Keeping students engaged through challenge cards, badges, recognition, and growth-mindset coaching.

- **TaxonomyID:** MOTIV
- **Concept count:** 30 (4.6% of total)

## Diversity Equity And Access (DEI)

Reaching underserved communities, removing cost and access barriers, and building an inclusive club.

- **TaxonomyID:** DEI
- **Concept count:** 26 (4.0% of total)

## Budget Funding And Partnerships (FUND)

Budgeting, fundraising, grant writing, and building partnerships that keep a club financially healthy.

- **TaxonomyID:** FUND
- **Concept count:** 36 (5.5% of total)

## AI Agents For Club Management (AI)

Using AI agents for registration, communication, scheduling, curriculum customization, and mentor coaching -- with appropriate oversight.

- **TaxonomyID:** AI
- **Concept count:** 50 (7.6% of total)

## Oversight And Sustainability (BOARD)

Oversight boards, inventory systems, advanced mentor recruiting, and the succession planning that makes a club outlast its founder.

- **TaxonomyID:** BOARD
- **Concept count:** 32 (4.9% of total)
