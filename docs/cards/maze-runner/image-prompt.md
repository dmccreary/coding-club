# Maze Runner Image Prompt

## Subject

An overhead (bird's-eye) view of a small square maze, built from thick maze
walls, with a bright green "Start" marker in the upper-left cell, a
black-and-white checkered "Finish" flag in the lower-right cell, and one
small round mouse character standing on the Start cell, facing into the
maze. Exactly one mouse, one Start marker, and one Finish flag must appear
-- no second mouse, no extra flags, no cheese or other props.

## Composition

Wide landscape canvas, 1774 x 887 pixels, a 2:1 aspect ratio (this card's
icon box renders a 2:1 image at 2.6in x 1.3in with no wasted padding). The
maze itself is a square game board, roughly centered vertically and
positioned left-of-center horizontally so it reads as the dominant shape;
comfortable white margin surrounds it on all sides. Clean white background,
no horizon, no tabletop edge, no surrounding room. Every element -- maze,
Start marker, Finish flag, mouse -- stays fully inside the frame.

## Maze grid and walls

The maze is drawn as an 8x8 arrangement of square cells (a simplified stand-in
for the program's full 32x32 grid -- eight cells per side reads clearly at
small print size, where 32 thin lines would blur together). Cell walls are
thick, rounded-end strokes in deep navy blue, forming corridors wide enough
to read clearly as open paths versus solid walls. The outer boundary of the
maze is a slightly heavier navy border. Interior walls are placed so the
maze has a clear, single winding corridor from the top-left cell to the
bottom-right cell -- some walls should be visibly "missing" to suggest an
open path, while most of the grid keeps enough walls standing to look like a
real maze, not open lattice. The floor of each open cell is plain white.

## Start marker

The top-left cell contains a small flat green square badge (medium green,
with a slightly darker green outline) with a simple white right-pointing
triangle (a "play" arrow shape) centered on it, immediately readable as
"Start" without needing the word spelled out.

## Finish flag

The bottom-right cell contains a small checkered flag on a short thin gray
flagpole, angled slightly as if waving. The flag's checkered pattern
alternates black and white squares in a simple 4x3 or 4x4 grid pattern. The
flag is compact enough to sit fully inside its single maze cell.

## Mouse character

A small, round, friendly cartoon mouse stands on the Start cell, body facing
toward the first open corridor. The mouse has warm gray fur, a lighter cream
underbelly, two large rounded ears (pale pink inner ear), small black bead
eyes, a short pink nose, and a thin curling pink tail trailing behind it. Its
proportions are simple and rounded -- no fingers, no visible feet detail
beyond small oval paw shapes -- matching a friendly, simplified educational
illustration style rather than a realistic rodent. The mouse is sized to fit
comfortably within one maze cell, clearly smaller than the maze cell itself
so it doesn't touch the surrounding walls.

## Style, color, and readability

Flat vector illustration with bold, consistent outlines and light,
simplified dimensional shading (soft highlights, no heavy gradients or
photorealistic texture). Primary palette: deep navy blue for maze walls,
white for open floor and background, medium green for the Start badge,
black and white for the Finish flag, and warm gray/cream/pink for the
mouse. This keeps every element legible when the illustration is printed
small on a folded card. Lighting reads as flat, even, overhead light with
no strong directional shadow.

## Avoid

No text, labels, numbers, or coordinate markings anywhere in the
illustration -- the Start badge and Finish flag must communicate their
meaning through shape and color alone. No people, hands, faces, or the
book's own mascot. No second mouse, no cheese, no extra decorative props.
No logos or watermarks. No photorealism -- flat/vector illustration only.
No drop shadow implying a 3D tabletop scene; keep the maze reading as a flat
overhead diagram.

## Detailed prompt

Create a polished educational illustration for a printed "Maze Runner"
challenge card. Use a wide landscape canvas with a 2:1 aspect ratio (1774 x
887 pixels) and a clean white background, with the finished maze positioned
left-of-center and comfortable white margin on every side.

Draw an overhead view of a square maze built from an 8x8 grid of cells, with
thick, rounded-end, deep-navy-blue wall strokes and a slightly heavier navy
outer border. Leave enough walls open to form one clear winding corridor
from the top-left cell to the bottom-right cell, while keeping most of the
grid's other walls standing so it still reads as a real maze. Keep every
open cell's floor plain white.

In the top-left cell, draw a small flat medium-green square badge with a
darker green outline and a centered white right-pointing triangle (a "play"
arrow), instantly readable as a Start marker. In the bottom-right cell, draw
a small checkered flag on a short gray pole, angled as if waving, with a
simple black-and-white 4x4 checkered pattern, sized to fit fully inside its
cell.

On the Start cell, draw one small round cartoon mouse facing into the maze:
warm gray fur, a cream underbelly, two large pink-inside ears, small black
bead eyes, a short pink nose, oval paw shapes, and a thin curling pink tail.
Keep its proportions simple and rounded, sized clearly smaller than one maze
cell so it never touches the surrounding walls.

Render everything as flat vector illustration with bold consistent outlines
and light, simplified shading -- no heavy gradients, no photorealism, no 3D
tabletop shadow. Use deep navy for the walls, medium green for the Start
badge, black and white for the Finish flag, and warm gray/cream/pink for the
mouse, all on a white background, so the whole scene stays legible when
printed small on a folded card. Do not add any text, labels, numbers,
people, hands, faces, the book's mascot, a second mouse, cheese, extra
props, logos, or watermarks.
