# Character Sheet: Circuit the Robot

The canonical identity document for Circuit, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Coding Clubs: How to Create, Organize, Promote and Manage a Sustainable
Coding Club** textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of AI-generated
content involving this character must re-anchor to the description below —
it is the source of truth for visual and voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Circuit
- **Species:** Robot
- **Subject:** Coding club operations, mentorship, physical computing, and AI-assisted club management
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's build something great!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Sky Blue — hex `#4A90D9`
- **Accent color:** Warm Amber — hex `#F5A623`
- **Clothing / accessories:** A small antenna topped with a glowing amber LED bulb; a rounded chassis with a soft digital screen face
- **Expression:** Friendly, warm screen-face with a simple curved smile display
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact, rounded proportions, approachable rather than imposing — like a friendly desktop helper robot
- **Art style:** Flat cartoon / vector illustration, clean lines, bold simple shapes

## Personality

- Warm
- Patient
- Encouraging
- A little bit goofy / playful

## Voice

- Uses simple, encouraging language
- Refers to club members as "builders" or "makers"
- Occasionally uses light electronics/circuit puns (e.g., "sparks of an idea," "let's connect the pieces")
- Signature phrases: "Let's build something great!", "Great connection!", "You're really wired for this!"

## Why This Mascot

A robot mascot directly mirrors the book's own content — robot kits,
Raspberry Pi Pico projects, and AI agents are central themes throughout —
while staying warm and approachable rather than cold or intimidating,
fitting a book written for volunteer mentors and club leaders rather than
engineers. Circuit's glowing antenna LED also echoes the Moving Rainbow
LED-strip kit that recurs across the physical computing chapters.
