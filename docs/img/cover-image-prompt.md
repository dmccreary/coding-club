# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

Coding Club is a textbook about how to create, organize, promote, and manage
a sustainable coding club for kids — covering everything from the first
club meeting and classroom layout through mentor recruiting, project kits,
registration systems, and using AI agents to reduce the administrative
workload. The intended audience is the **adults** who run these clubs:
teachers, librarians, parents, and engineers volunteering as mentors —
not the students who attend them. The visual tone should be warm,
organized, and encouraging, with a "professional development for
volunteers" feel rather than a kids'-cartoon feel — approachable enough
to invite a first-time volunteer in, but composed enough to read as a
serious operations guide.

## Title

Place "Coding Club" in the center of the image, in a clean, highly legible
sans-serif font. Use a light/white font color with a subtle drop shadow or
dark scrim behind it so it stays readable against the busy montage
background. Keep the title short enough to render at a large size — do not
shrink it to fit if the title is long; instead simplify the background
directly behind the text.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 9 concepts around the title, each
rendered in a consistent illustration style (see Style below) so the
composition reads as one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles:

- **Moving Rainbow LED Strip Kit** — a short strip of NeoPixel LEDs glowing
  in a smooth rainbow gradient, mounted on a small breadboard with a
  Raspberry Pi Pico and a couple of visible jumper wires.
- **Center-Table Classroom Layout** — a simple top-down or angled view of a
  classroom with monitors arranged facing inward around a center table,
  small figures of mentors and students seated around it.
- **Challenge Cards** — a small fanned stack of colorful printed challenge
  cards standing upright in a card holder on a table, each card showing a
  simple icon and a short title.
- **Robot Kit** — a small tabletop collision-avoidance robot with wheels
  and a front sensor, sitting next to its open kit box.
- **3:1 Mentor-to-Student Ratio** — a friendly adult mentor leaning in to
  help three young students gathered around a single laptop.
- **AI Agent Dashboard** — a laptop or tablet screen showing a friendly,
  simple chat-assistant icon alongside a registration list and a calendar
  icon, suggesting AI-assisted club management.
- **Badges and Stickers** — a small sheet of round achievement badges and
  stickers with simple circuit and star icons.
- **Inventory Kit Box with QR Code** — a labeled storage bin or kit box
  with a printed QR code sticker on its side.
- **Breadboard Physical Computing** — a Raspberry Pi Pico on a breadboard
  with a single blinking LED and a push button, jumper wires in the
  mascot's amber and sky-blue accent colors.

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, Circuit the Robot, in the lower-left corner, sized
so it does not overlap the title text. Circuit is a small, rounded,
friendly desktop-helper-style robot: sky blue body (`#4A90D9`), warm amber
accents (`#F5A623`), a small antenna topped with a glowing amber LED bulb,
and a rounded chassis with a soft digital screen face showing a simple
curved smile. Circuit's proportions are compact and approachable, drawn in
a flat cartoon / vector illustration style with clean lines and bold simple
shapes — see the attached reference image (`docs/img/mascot/welcome.png`)
for exact appearance and match it precisely.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: flat vector illustration with clean lines and bold
  simple shapes — matching the mascot's own art style so the montage and
  the mascot read as one consistent world, not two different styles pasted
  together.
- Color palette: Sky Blue (`#4A90D9`) and Warm Amber (`#F5A623`), drawn
  directly from the mascot's colors, on a warm off-white or soft light-gray
  background so the palette stays clean rather than saturated.
- Lighting/mood: bright, optimistic, and welcoming — like the cover of a
  well-organized volunteer handbook, not a dark or high-tech aesthetic.
- Composition: title centered, montage elements arranged in a loose ring or
  grid around it, Circuit in the lower-left, generous negative space
  immediately behind the title so it stays readable.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, people
  pointing at whiteboards) unless one is explicitly one of the chosen
  concepts.
- Avoid photorealistic human faces unless the book's subject calls for a
  specific recognizable figure — keep any human figures in the same flat
  vector style as the mascot.
- Avoid depicting students as the primary focus — the audience for this
  book is the adult club leaders and mentors, so any human figures should
  emphasize mentors/organizers over students.
- Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the title.
