# E-Ink Displays

!!! note "Photo needed"
    We don't have a photo of this kit yet.

Notes on low-cost e-paper displays with good MicroPython drivers, such as the small Waveshare modules and LILYGO T5 boards.

**Source book:** Clocks and Watches  
**Kit page:** [https://dmccreary.github.io/clocks-and-watches/kits/e-paper/](https://dmccreary.github.io/clocks-and-watches/kits/e-paper/)  
**Printable one-pager:** [e-paper-one-pager.html](e-paper-one-pager.html)

## Features

- Uses the ESP32 Boards
- Uses the Waveshare SSD1680 E-Paper

## Benefits

- Free, open lessons: the Clocks and Watches textbook has the kit page, wiring, and MicroPython code.
- Instant feedback: students see their code's results on lights or a display.
- Budget: about $19 (1.54" Waveshare) to $28 (2.9" Waveshare) for the display alone on eBay.

## Parts

- **ESP32** — Microcontroller with Wireless
- **Waveshare SSD1680 E-Paper** — Display
- **830-Point Breadboard ($1.10)** — Breadboard
- **Micro-USB Data Cable ($2.50)** — Power and programming cable
