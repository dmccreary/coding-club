# Chrono Smart Clock Kit

![Chrono Smart Clock Kit](clocks-and-watches-sw-gc9b72.jpg){ width="420" }

A round-screen smart clock on a Pico W that sets its own time from the internet and shows the weather, a stopwatch, and a timer. It has 13 labs, needs no soldering, and is for ages 10 and up.

**Source book:** Clocks and Watches  
**Kit page:** [https://dmccreary.github.io/clocks-and-watches/kits/sw-gc9b72/](https://dmccreary.github.io/clocks-and-watches/kits/sw-gc9b72/)  
**Printable one-pager:** [clocks-and-watches-sw-gc9b72-one-pager.html](clocks-and-watches-sw-gc9b72-one-pager.html)

## Features

- Set its own time. It asks the internet what time it is, so it is never
- Show the time your way. Pick giant digital numbers or an old-style
- Tell you the weather. Today's and tomorrow's forecast appear with
- Time laps and count down. It works as a stopwatch that records laps
- Switch with one button. Press MODE to step through all five faces.

## Benefits

- Free, open lessons: the Clocks and Watches textbook has the kit page, wiring, and MicroPython code.
- Ready-made curriculum: 14 additional lab pages walk students through the build step by step.
- Classroom friendly: built on a solderless breadboard or no-solder connectors.
- Wireless-ready: the Pico W option adds Wi-Fi for time sync and web projects.
- Instant feedback: students see their code's results on lights or a display.
- Budget: under $20.

## Parts

- **Raspberry Pi Pico W ($5)** — Microcontroller with Wireless
- **GC9A01 Round TFT ($3.19)** — Display
- **GC9B72 Smartwatch Display ($5.39)** — Display
- **Momentary Push Buttons** — Input button / switch
- **Piezo Buzzer / Speaker** — Sound output
- **830-Point Breadboard ($1.10)** — Breadboard
- **Micro-USB Data Cable ($2.50)** — Power and programming cable
