# MAX98357A I2S Amplifier Kit

![MAX98357A I2S Amplifier Kit](max98357a-amp.jpg){ width="420" }

Replaces the robot's single-tone piezo beep with a class-D amplifier chip that drives a real speaker loud enough to fill a room on three AA batteries.

**Source book:** STEM Robots  
**Kit page:** [https://dmccreary.github.io/stem-robots/kits/max98357a-amp/](https://dmccreary.github.io/stem-robots/kits/max98357a-amp/)  
**Printable one-pager:** [max98357a-amp-one-pager.html](max98357a-amp-one-pager.html)

## Benefits

- Free, open lessons: the STEM Robots textbook has the kit page, wiring, and MicroPython code.
- Ready-made curriculum: 6 additional lab pages walk students through the build step by step.
- Classroom friendly: built on a solderless breadboard or no-solder connectors.
- Instant feedback: students see their code's results on lights or a display.
- Budget: about $11–$15 (amplifier about $8–$12 on eBay, small 8 ohm speaker about $3).

## Parts

- **Cytron Maker Pi RP2040 ($10)** — Controller board (built-in RP2040)
- **10K Potentiometer** — Input knob
- **8 Ω 50 mm Speaker** — Sound output
- **TT DC Gear Motors** — Motor / actuator
- **GC9A01 Round TFT ($3.19)** — Display
- **MAX98357A I2S Amplifier ($0.99)** — Audio amplifier
- **Momentary Push Buttons** — Input button / switch
- **Piezo Buzzer / Speaker** — Sound output
- **SG90 Micro Servo** — Motor / actuator
- **830-Point Breadboard ($1.10)** — Breadboard
- **Micro-USB Data Cable ($2.50)** — Power and programming cable
