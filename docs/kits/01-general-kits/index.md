Dan's survey of commercial MicroPython starter kits, most built on the Raspberry Pi Pico or Pico 2, plus a few ESP32 kits for wireless projects. It compares kits such as the Seeed Grove Starter Kit ($40) and several eBay Pico kits.

**Source book:** Learning MicroPython

## Features

- Lab: NeoPixel Two Button Kit
- Lab: Rotary NeoPixel
- Lab: MicroBit
- Lab: MicroPython Projects
- Lab: Sourcing Parts

## Benefits

- Free, open lessons: the Learning MicroPython textbook has the kit page, wiring, and MicroPython code.
- Ready-made curriculum: 5 additional lab pages walk students through the build step by step.
- Classroom friendly: built on a solderless breadboard or no-solder connectors.
- Wireless-ready: the Pico W option adds Wi-Fi for time sync and web projects.
- Instant feedback: students see their code's results on lights or a display.
- Budget: $32–$125, depending on the kit.
