# Holiday Hats

!!! note "Photo needed"
    We don't have a photo of this kit yet.

A favorite student project: a foam-core hat lit with the standard Moving Rainbow kit. Students reuse the same hat all year by changing the color pattern.

**Source book:** Moving Rainbow  
**Kit page:** [https://dmccreary.github.io/moving-rainbow/kits/holiday-hats/](https://dmccreary.github.io/moving-rainbow/kits/holiday-hats/)  
**Printable one-pager:** [holiday-hats-one-pager.html](holiday-hats-one-pager.html)

## Features

- Lab: 4th of July Hat
- Lab: Christmas Hat
- Lab: Halloween Hat
- Lab: St. Patrick's Day Hat
- Lab: Generating LED Patterns for a Valentines Day Hat

## Benefits

- Free, open lessons: the Moving Rainbow textbook has the kit page, wiring, and MicroPython code.
- Ready-made curriculum: 5 additional lab pages walk students through the build step by step.
- Budget: about $15 (the Moving Rainbow base kit) plus a sheet of foam-core board.

## Parts

- **Raspberry Pi Pico ($4)** — Microcontroller
- **WS2812B NeoPixels** — LEDs
- **830-Point Breadboard ($1.10)** — Breadboard
- **Micro-USB Data Cable ($2.50)** — Power and programming cable
