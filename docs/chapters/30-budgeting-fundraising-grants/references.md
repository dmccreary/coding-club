# References: Budgeting, Fundraising, and Grant Writing

1. [Grant writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grant_writing) - Wikipedia - Overview of how organizations request funding from foundations and government sources, the process behind this chapter's grant-writing-basics section.

2. [Crowdfunding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowdfunding) - Wikipedia - Explains how many small contributions fund a project through an online platform, the model behind this chapter's crowdfunding-campaign section.

3. [Fiscal sponsorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiscal_sponsorship) - Wikipedia - Explains how a project can accept tax-deductible donations through an established nonprofit's exempt status, the arrangement behind this chapter's nonprofit-fiscal-sponsorship section.

4. Storytelling for Grantseekers: A Guide to Creative Nonprofit Fundraising (3rd Edition) - Cheryl A. Clarke - Jossey-Bass - Clarke is credited with a narrative-driven approach to grant proposal writing, structuring a case for support around a clear story, the framework behind this chapter's grant-proposal-structure section.

5. Financial Leadership for Nonprofit Executives - Jeanne Bell, Jan Masaoka, and Steve Zimmerman - Fieldstone Alliance - This widely used guide connects mission to money for small nonprofits, including line-item budgeting and cost-per-participant estimates, the reasoning behind this chapter's establishing-a-budget and cost-per-student-estimate sections.

6. [Crowdfunding Your Classroom 101](https://blog.donorschoose.org/articles/crowdfunding-for-classrooms-101) - DonorsChoose - Explains how a teacher-specific crowdfunding platform lets educators request exactly the materials they need, a working model for this chapter's crowdfunding-campaign section.

7. [A Board's Guide to Fiscal Sponsorship](https://propelnonprofits.org/resources/a-boards-guide-to-fiscal-sponsorship/) - Propel Nonprofits - Explains how a club too small to incorporate can operate under an established nonprofit's tax-exempt status, directly supporting this chapter's nonprofit-fiscal-sponsorship section.

8. [NNFS Guidelines for Comprehensive Fiscal Sponsorship](https://www.fiscalsponsors.org/nnfs-guidelines) - National Network of Fiscal Sponsors - The field's best-practice standards for fiscal sponsorship arrangements, supporting this chapter's nonprofit-fiscal-sponsorship section.

9. [Insurance Basics for Nonprofits](https://nonprofitrisk.org/resources/insurance-basics-for-nonprofits/) - Nonprofit Risk Management Center - Background on formalizing an organization's finances, relevant to this chapter's establishing-a-budget and strategic-partnership-building sections.

10. [Free Equipment Inventory Templates & Spreadsheets](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/equipment-inventory-templates) - Smartsheet - Spreadsheet layouts adaptable into a recurring-and-one-time-cost budget tracker, supporting this chapter's budget-line-item and recurring-cost-planning sections.
