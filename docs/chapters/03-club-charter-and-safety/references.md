# References: Writing Your Club Charter and Safety Policies

1. [By-law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/By-law) - Wikipedia - Explains what bylaws are and how organizations use them to formalize roles, decision-making, and amendment procedures, the structure this chapter's club charter builds on.

2. [Background check](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Background_check) - Wikipedia - Overview of criminal-history and identity screening processes, the mechanism behind the volunteer background check policy this chapter requires before anyone mentors minors.

3. [Code of conduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_of_conduct) - Wikipedia - Explains how organizations codify expected behavior and enforcement, directly relevant to the club's code of conduct and anti-harassment policy.

4. Boards That Make a Difference (3rd Edition) - John Carver - Jossey-Bass - Carver originated the Policy Governance model, which separates an organization's foundational "ends" policies from day-to-day operating rules, the same split this chapter uses to structure a club charter versus its bylaws.

5. Robert's Rules of Order Newly Revised - Henry M. Robert - Da Capo Press - Robert's 1876 manual remains the standard reference for the voting, motions, and conflict-resolution procedures small organizations like a coding club adopt in their bylaws and decision-making process.

6. [Screening and Background Checks](https://safekidsthrive.org/the-report/key-sections/section-2-screening-and-background-checks-for-selecting-employees-and-volunteers/) - Safe Kids Thrive (Children's Trust of Massachusetts) - A practical framework for screening staff and volunteers who work with children, covering the risk-based approach this chapter's background-check policy follows.

7. [Nonprofit Bylaws](https://boardsource.org/resources/nonprofit-bylaws/) - BoardSource - Explains what belongs in a small organization's bylaws, how they differ from a mission or charter, and common drafting mistakes, supporting this chapter's charter-writing walkthrough.

8. [Youth Program Sample Code of Conduct](https://nonprofitrisk.org/resources/youth-program-sample-code-of-conduct/) - Nonprofit Risk Management Center - A ready-to-adapt code of conduct template for youth-serving organizations, listing prohibited behaviors and enforcement expectations a club can adopt directly.

9. [Children's Privacy](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/childrens-privacy) - Federal Trade Commission - Federal guidance on COPPA and protecting children's personal information online, the legal backdrop for the data privacy policy this chapter asks clubs to write.

10. [Photo Release Template](https://nonprofithub.org/photo-release-template/) - Nonprofit Hub - A free, fill-in template for collecting photo and video consent, the paperwork this chapter's photo release form section walks readers through.
