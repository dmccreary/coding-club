# References: Running a Session: Room Layout to Cleanup

1. [Blended learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blended_learning) - Wikipedia - Explains the station rotation model and other blended-learning structures that mix small-group, independent, and teacher-led time, the pattern behind this chapter's station-based session design.

2. [First aid kit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_aid_kit) - Wikipedia - Describes what a first aid kit contains and how it is organized, supporting this chapter's guidance on locating and stocking one for every session.

3. [Emergency exit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_exit) - Wikipedia - Covers how buildings mark and route occupants to exits during an emergency, the awareness this chapter asks every mentor to build before a session starts.

4. Blended Learning in Grades 4-12: Leveraging the Power of Technology to Create Student-Centered Classrooms - Catlin Tucker - Corwin Press - Tucker is credited with popularizing and refining the Station Rotation model of small-group instruction, the station-based learning structure this chapter's session walkthrough is built around.

5. The First Days of School: How to Be an Effective Teacher - Harry K. Wong and Rosemary T. Wong - Harry K. Wong Publications - The Wongs are the most widely cited authors on classroom procedures and routines, including consistent warm-up, transition, and wrap-up rituals, the backbone of this chapter's session walkthrough.

6. [The Station Rotation Model](https://catlintucker.com/2021/10/station-rotation-model/) - Dr. Catlin Tucker - A practitioner's own explanation of teacher-led, online, and offline stations, giving a concrete model for this chapter's small-group rotation and station-based learning sections.

7. [Make a First Aid Kit: What to Include](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/anatomy-of-a-first-aid-kit.html) - American Red Cross - An itemized checklist for assembling a first aid kit, supporting this chapter's guidance on stocking and locating one in the session room.

8. [Accessible Meetings, Events & Conferences Guide](https://www.adainfo.org/hospitality/accessible-meetings-events-conferences-guide/) - Mid-Atlantic ADA Center - Practical checklists for accessible room layouts, seating, and emergency planning, supporting this chapter's accessibility-of-space guidance.

9. [14 Effective Opening and Closing Routines for Teachers](https://www.edutopia.org/article/14-effective-opening-and-closing-routines-for-teachers) - Edutopia - Concrete warm-up and wrap-up activities teachers use to bookend a lesson, directly supporting this chapter's session warm-up and wrap-up sections.

10. [Screening and Background Checks](https://safekidsthrive.org/the-report/key-sections/section-2-screening-and-background-checks-for-selecting-employees-and-volunteers/) - Safe Kids Thrive (Children's Trust of Massachusetts) - Covers visitor and volunteer check-in as part of a layered child-safety approach, supporting this chapter's visitor check-in and photography-during-session policies.
