# References: AI Agents for Registration, Scheduling, and Communication

1. [Intelligent agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligent_agent) - Wikipedia - Defines a system that perceives its environment and acts to achieve goals, the foundational concept behind this chapter's AI-agent-basics section.

2. [Chatbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot) - Wikipedia - History and design of conversational software, the technology behind this chapter's AI-chatbot-for-parents and AI-chatbot-for-students sections.

3. [Sentiment analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentiment_analysis) - Wikipedia - Explains how software classifies the emotional tone of text, the technique behind this chapter's AI-sentiment-analysis section for reading survey and message feedback.

4. Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach (4th Edition) - Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig - Pearson - Russell and Norvig wrote the field's standard textbook, credited with the canonical definition of an intelligent agent perceiving and acting on its environment, the concept behind this chapter's AI-agent-basics section.

5. Weapons of Math Destruction: How Big Data Increases Inequality and Threatens Democracy - Cathy O'Neil - Crown - O'Neil is widely credited with popularizing the case for human oversight of automated decision systems, the reasoning behind this chapter's emphasis on guardrails and keeping a mentor in the loop.

6. [Getting Started on a Responsible AI Use Policy for Nonprofits](https://candid.org/blogs/how-to-create-responsible-ai-use-policy-for-nonprofits/) - Candid - Practical guidance on scope, data handling, and prohibited uses for a small organization's AI policy, directly supporting this chapter's human-in-the-loop and oversight guidance.

7. [What Are AI Agents?](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-agents) - IBM - Explains the plan-act-review loop that distinguishes an autonomous AI agent from a simple chatbot, directly supporting this chapter's AI-agent-basics section.

8. [How to Segment Your Email List](https://mailchimp.com/resources/email-segmentation/) - Mailchimp - The targeting logic behind this chapter's influence-graph concept for reaching specific mentor populations with the right message.

9. [How to Write Survey Questions That Get Better Responses](https://www.surveymonkey.com/learn/research-and-analysis/writing-survey-questions/) - SurveyMonkey - Groundwork for the AI-survey-analysis section, since a well-designed survey produces the clean data an AI agent needs to summarize accurately.

10. [Khanmigo](https://www.khanmigo.ai/) - Khan Academy - A widely used education AI assistant built around Socratic guidance rather than direct answers, a real-world model for this chapter's mentor-coaching-AI-agent section.
