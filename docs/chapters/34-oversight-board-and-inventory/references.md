1. [Board of directors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_directors) - Wikipedia - Explains the role and legal responsibilities of a governing board, the foundation for this chapter's oversight-board and board-roles-and-duties sections.

2. [Succession planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Succession_planning) - Wikipedia - Covers how organizations prepare for planned leadership transitions, the concept behind this chapter's succession-planning section.

3. [Barcode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcode) - Wikipedia - Explains how machine-readable codes, including QR codes, encode data for scanning, the technology behind this chapter's inventory-sticker-printing and QR-code-on-kits sections.

4. Boards That Make a Difference (3rd Edition) - John Carver - Jossey-Bass - Carver's Policy Governance model, distinguishing board oversight from day-to-day operations, is the direct framework behind this chapter's oversight-board-roles-and-duties section.

5. Managing Transitions: Making the Most of Change - William Bridges - Da Capo Lifelong Books - Bridges is widely credited with the standard framework for navigating organizational transitions, the reasoning behind this chapter's succession-planning and board-term-limits sections.

6. [Board Member Roles and Responsibilities](https://boardsource.org/fundamental-topics-of-nonprofit-board-service/roles-responsibilities/) - BoardSource - Defines the distinct duties of an oversight board's officers and members, directly supporting this chapter's board-roles-and-duties section.

7. [How to Use QR Codes for Asset Management](https://www.sortly.com/blog/asset-management-qr-code/) - Sortly - Explains how QR codes speed up checkout and reduce errors compared to manual logs, directly supporting this chapter's equipment-checkout-system and asset-tagging-process sections.

8. [Free Equipment Inventory Templates & Spreadsheets](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/equipment-inventory-templates) - Smartsheet - Spreadsheet layouts adaptable into an inventory-audit and equipment-return-tracking log, supporting this chapter's club-inventory-system section.

9. [Start a Club](https://girlswhocode.com/get-involved/start-a-club) - Girls Who Code - A national network's approach to recruiting and screening mentors at scale, supporting this chapter's advanced-mentor-recruiting section.

10. [Elements of Effective Practice for Mentoring](https://eepm.mentoring.org/) - MENTOR: National Mentoring Partnership - Standards for finding and vetting qualified mentors, directly supporting this chapter's finding-qualified-mentors section.
