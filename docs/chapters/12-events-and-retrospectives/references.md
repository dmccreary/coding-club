# References: Running Events and Learning From Retrospectives

1. [After-action review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/After-action_review) - Wikipedia - Explains the structured, blame-free debrief technique originally developed by the U.S. Army, the model behind this chapter's event-debriefing and retrospective practice.

2. [QR code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_code) - Wikipedia - Explains how QR codes encode and scan information, the technology behind this chapter's QR-code check-in system.

3. [Sprint retrospective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrum_(software_development)) - Wikipedia - Covers the Scrum framework's end-of-cycle review meeting, the software-industry practice this chapter adapts into a repeatable event retrospective.

4. Agile Retrospectives: Making Good Teams Great - Esther Derby and Diana Larsen - Pragmatic Bookshelf - Derby and Larsen popularized the modern five-stage retrospective format (set the stage, gather data, generate insights, decide, close) now used across industries, the structure behind this chapter's event-retrospective checklist.

5. Internet, Mail, and Mixed-Mode Surveys: The Tailored Design Method - Don A. Dillman, Jolene D. Smyth, and Leah Melani Christian - Wiley - Dillman is the most widely cited authority on survey design methodology, the "Tailored Design Method" behind this chapter's post-event survey question design and response-analysis guidance.

6. [Check in Attendees at Your Event](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/741083/how-to-check-in-attendees-at-the-event-with-eventbrite-organizer/) - Eventbrite Help Center - Instructions for scanning QR codes and manually checking in attendees, directly supporting this chapter's session check-in system.

7. [Sprint Retrospective: How to Hold an Effective Meeting](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/retrospective) - Atlassian - A five-step facilitation guide and discussion techniques like "4 Ls," adaptable to this chapter's event debriefing meeting.

8. [How to Write Survey Questions That Get Better Responses](https://www.surveymonkey.com/learn/research-and-analysis/writing-survey-questions/) - SurveyMonkey - Practical tips for clear, neutral survey questions, supporting this chapter's post-event survey design section.

9. [Set and Manage Your Refund Policy](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/130304/how-to-set-a-refund-policy/) - Eventbrite Help Center - Explains how to configure refund windows and cancellation terms, directly supporting this chapter's cancellation and refund policy guidance.

10. [Afterschool Alliance](https://afterschoolalliance.org/) - Afterschool Alliance - National resources on summer and multi-session programming, supporting this chapter's summer-camp-format and multi-session-series-planning sections.
