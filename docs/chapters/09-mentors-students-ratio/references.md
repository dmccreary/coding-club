# References: Mentors, Students, and the 3:1 Ratio

1. [Mentorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentorship) - Wikipedia - Overview of mentoring relationships and their forms, including volunteer, professional, and peer mentoring, the three mentor types this chapter defines.

2. [Peer mentoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_mentoring) - Wikipedia - Explains how same-age or near-age mentors support learning, directly relevant to this chapter's peer mentor category and mixed-age grouping guidance.

3. [Student–teacher ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student%E2%80%93teacher_ratio) - Wikipedia - Explains how the ratio of instructors to learners affects attention and outcomes, the research background behind this chapter's 3:1 student-to-mentor recommendation.

4. Handbook of Youth Mentoring (2nd Edition) - David L. DuBois and Michael J. Karcher (Editors) - Sage Publications - DuBois and Karcher compiled the standard scholarly reference on youth mentoring practice, including research on the mentor-to-student ratios that keep sessions effective, the evidence behind this chapter's 3:1 recommendation.

5. The Montessori Method - Maria Montessori - Frederick A. Stokes Company - Montessori pioneered mixed-age classroom grouping, showing how older and younger students learning side by side benefit both, the model behind this chapter's mixed-age-grouping and sibling-pairing guidance.

6. [Elements of Effective Practice for Mentoring](https://eepm.mentoring.org/) - MENTOR: National Mentoring Partnership - The field's standard framework of 16 elements for building an effective mentoring program, supporting this chapter's guidance on adding, matching, and tracking mentors responsibly.

7. [Programs](https://www.bbbs.org/programs/) - Big Brothers Big Sisters of America - Describes how a national mentoring organization structures and matches mentor-student pairs, a real-world reference point for this chapter's mentor-skill-matching guidance.

8. [Wisdom of the (Multi) Ages: Students Learn by Teaching](https://www.edutopia.org/multiage-classroom-looping-peer-mentoring) - Edutopia - Practical strategies for pairing students across ages and skill levels so both the mentor and mentee benefit, supporting this chapter's peer-mentor and mixed-age-grouping sections.

9. [Multi-age classroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-age_classroom) - Wikipedia - Covers the research and history behind grouping students of different ages together, further grounding this chapter's mixed-age-grouping and sibling-pairing guidance.

10. [Start a Club](https://girlswhocode.com/get-involved/start-a-club) - Girls Who Code - Documents the volunteer and professional mentor roles a real coding-club network recruits and screens, illustrating the mentor categories this chapter defines.
