# References: Tracking Student Data and Managing Club Communication

1. [Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Educational_Rights_and_Privacy_Act) - Wikipedia - Explains the federal law protecting student education records, the legal backdrop for this chapter's student-data-privacy section.

2. [Data retention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_retention) - Wikipedia - Covers how organizations decide what data to keep and for how long, the concept behind this chapter's data-retention-policy section.

3. [Electronic mailing list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_mailing_list) - Wikipedia - Explains how organizations manage group email communication, the background for this chapter's email-list-segmentation and communication-tools sections.

4. Privacy by Design: The 7 Foundational Principles - Ann Cavoukian - Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario - Cavoukian originated the "Privacy by Design" framework, embedding privacy protection into a system from the start rather than adding it later, the minimal-data-first approach this chapter's student-data-privacy section follows.

5. The Nonprofit Marketing Guide - Kivi Leroux Miller - Jossey-Bass - Miller's practical guidance on segmenting email lists and choosing communication tools for small organizations directly supports this chapter's email-list-segmentation and communication-tools sections.

6. [FERPA](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa) - U.S. Department of Education, Student Privacy Policy Office - The federal government's own overview of education-record privacy rights, directly supporting this chapter's saving-student-data and student-data-privacy sections.

7. [How to Segment Your Email List](https://mailchimp.com/resources/email-segmentation/) - Mailchimp - A step-by-step process for grouping subscribers by criteria like grade level or role, directly supporting this chapter's email-list-segmentation section.

8. [Use a Family Calendar on Google](https://support.google.com/families/answer/7157782) - Google Families Help - Official instructions for a shared calendar families and mentors can both see, supporting this chapter's shared-calendar-access section.

9. [Create Forms](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6281888) - Google Docs Editors Help - Google's official instructions for building feedback forms and collecting responses, the free tool this chapter recommends for its forms-and-feedback-tools section.

10. [Free Google Sheets Inventory Templates](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/google-sheets-inventory-templates) - Smartsheet - Spreadsheet template designs adaptable into a simple student-progress tracker, supporting this chapter's simple-spreadsheet-tracking and spreadsheet-template-design sections.
