# References: Robots, USB Basics, and Electronics Workshop Skills

1. [USB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USB) - Wikipedia - History and technical overview of the Universal Serial Bus standard, the foundation for this chapter's USB cable and connector basics.

2. [USB-C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USB-C) - Wikipedia - Explains the reversible USB-C connector and its power-delivery capabilities, directly relevant to this chapter's USB-A-versus-USB-C comparison.

3. [Electronic waste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_waste) - Wikipedia - Covers the environmental and safety issues around discarded electronics, the background for this chapter's safe-disposal-of-electronics section.

4. Electronics Cookbook: Practical Electronic Recipes with Arduino and Raspberry Pi - Simon Monk - O'Reilly Media - Monk is a widely credited, prolific author of practical maker-electronics guides, known for the component-identification and troubleshooting recipes this chapter's workshop-skills section draws on.

5. The Art of Electronics (3rd Edition) - Paul Horowitz and Winfield Hill - Cambridge University Press - Horowitz and Hill wrote the field's standard reference on reading datasheets and troubleshooting circuits, the authority behind this chapter's simplified datasheet-reading and circuit-troubleshooting guidance.

6. [SparkFun Troubleshooting Tips](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/sparkfun-troubleshooting-tips/all) - SparkFun Learn - A systematic checklist for diagnosing wiring, power, and software problems, directly supporting this chapter's circuit-troubleshooting section.

7. [Beginner Parts Kit Identification Guide](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/beginner-parts-kit-identification-guide/all) - SparkFun Learn - Photographs and descriptions for identifying common electronic components, supporting this chapter's component-identification section.

8. [EOS/ESD Fundamentals](https://www.esda.org/esd-overview/esd-fundamentals/) - EOS/ESD Association - Explains how static electricity damages sensitive components and how grounding prevents it, the standard behind this chapter's static-safe-handling guidance.

9. [Electronics Donation and Recycling](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/electronics-donation-and-recycling) - U.S. Environmental Protection Agency - Federal guidance on where and how to responsibly recycle or donate old electronics, directly supporting this chapter's safe-disposal-of-electronics section.

10. [How to Read a Schematic](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/how-to-read-a-schematic/reading-schematics) - SparkFun Learn - A beginner's guide to circuit symbols and diagram conventions, extending this book's circuit-diagram-reading skill into the prototyping-board and datasheet-reading topics in this chapter.
