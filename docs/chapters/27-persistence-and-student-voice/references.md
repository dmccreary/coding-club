# References: Encouraging Persistence and Student Voice

1. [Grit (personality trait)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grit_(personality_trait)) - Wikipedia - Explains the combination of passion and sustained perseverance toward long-term goals, the research behind this chapter's persistence-coaching section.

2. [Student voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_voice) - Wikipedia - Covers how giving learners input into their own education improves engagement, the concept behind this chapter's student-voice-and-choice section.

3. [Goal setting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_setting) - Wikipedia - Explains how specific, structured goals improve performance and persistence, the background for this chapter's student-goal-setting section.

4. Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance - Angela Duckworth - Scribner - Duckworth originated grit research, identifying passion and sustained perseverance toward long-term goals as key predictors of success, the framework behind this chapter's persistence-coaching and failure-as-learning-tool sections.

5. Lifelong Kindergarten: Cultivating Creativity through Projects, Passion, Peers, and Play - Mitchel Resnick - MIT Press - Resnick's "Passion" principle — letting learners choose projects tied to their own interests — is the direct source for this chapter's passion-project-time and personal-project-choice sections.

6. [The Right Kind of Praise Can Spur Student Growth](https://www.edutopia.org/article/right-kind-praise-can-spur-student-growth/) - Edutopia - Research-backed guidance on process-focused, measured praise over generic compliments, directly supporting this chapter's mentor-praise-technique and encouragement-language sections.

7. [Using Genius Time/Passion Projects to Encourage Exploration of Computer Science](https://advocate.csteachers.org/2018/08/30/using-genius-time-passion-projects-to-encourage-exploration-of-computer-science/) - Computer Science Teachers Association - Explains how self-directed exploration time builds interest in computer science specifically, directly supporting this chapter's passion-project-time section.

8. [What Is Genius Hour and How Can I Try It in My Classroom?](https://www.weareteachers.com/what-is-genius-hour/) - WeAreTeachers - A practical walkthrough of structuring student-chosen project time, supporting this chapter's personal-project-choice and student-interest-survey sections.

9. [A Simple Tool for Fostering Growth Mindset](https://www.edutopia.org/article/simple-tool-fostering-growth-mindset) - Edutopia - A classroom practice for treating mistakes as visible learning steps, supporting this chapter's failure-as-learning-tool section.

10. [Using Gamification in Grades 3 to 12 to Ignite Student Learning](https://www.edutopia.org/article/using-gamification-ignite-student-learning/) - Edutopia - Practical guidance on point systems and level-up structures that reward participation over rank, directly supporting this chapter's gamification-elements and point-system-design sections.
