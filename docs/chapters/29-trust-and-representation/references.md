# References: Building Trust and Representation in Your Club

1. [Culturally relevant pedagogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culturally_relevant_pedagogy) - Wikipedia - Explains teaching practices that connect instruction to students' cultural backgrounds, the framework behind this chapter's culturally-responsive-teaching section.

2. [Assistive technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistive_technology) - Wikipedia - Covers tools and adaptations that support learners with disabilities, the background for this chapter's assistive-technology-option section.

3. [Role model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role_model) - Wikipedia - Explains how seeing someone similar to oneself succeed shapes aspiration and confidence, the concept behind this chapter's role-model-visibility section.

4. The Dreamkeepers: Successful Teachers of African American Children - Gloria Ladson-Billings - Jossey-Bass - Ladson-Billings originated culturally relevant pedagogy, identifying the teaching practices that build trust and engagement across cultural lines, the framework behind this chapter's culturally-responsive-teaching section.

5. Why Are All the Black Kids Sitting Together in the Cafeteria? - Beverly Daniel Tatum - Basic Books - Tatum's widely cited work on racial identity development informs this chapter's guidance on representation in curriculum, role-model visibility, and bias-awareness training.

6. [Tools & Resources for 7 Actionable Principles](https://7principles.thecne.org/tools-and-resources/) - Center for Nonprofit Excellence - A curated library of equity-focused self-assessment tools for nonprofits, directly supporting this chapter's equity-audit-of-club section.

7. [Faith and Community Engagement](https://www.samhsa.gov/communities/faith-based-community-engagement) - Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration - Federal guidance on building trust and partnerships with faith-based organizations, directly supporting this chapter's faith-based-venue-partnership section.

8. [UDL Guidelines](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) - CAST - Available in over 20 languages, supporting this chapter's assistive-technology-option and inclusive-marketing-materials sections.

9. [Start a Club](https://girlswhocode.com/get-involved/start-a-club) - Girls Who Code - A national model for female-mentor recruitment and role-model visibility in coding programs, supporting this chapter's female-mentor-recruitment section.

10. [Afterschool Alliance](https://afterschoolalliance.org/) - Afterschool Alliance - National data on rural and urban program access gaps, supporting this chapter's rural-community-outreach and urban-community-outreach sections.
