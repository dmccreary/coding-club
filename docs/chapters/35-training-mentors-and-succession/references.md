# References: Training Mentors and Building a Club That Outlasts You

1. [Standard operating procedure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_operating_procedure) - Wikipedia - Explains how documented, repeatable procedures preserve institutional knowledge, the concept behind this chapter's standard-operating-procedure and documenting-club-procedures sections.

2. [Knowledge transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_transfer) - Wikipedia - Covers how expertise moves from one person or generation of leaders to the next, the process behind this chapter's knowledge-transfer-process and new-leader-onboarding sections.

3. [Strategic planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_planning) - Wikipedia - Explains how organizations set long-range goals and the steps to reach them, the background for this chapter's annual-strategic-plan and multi-year-vision-plan sections.

4. The Checklist Manifesto: How to Get Things Right - Atul Gawande - Metropolitan Books - Gawande popularized the checklist as a tool for capturing expert knowledge into a repeatable procedure, the reasoning behind this chapter's standard-operating-procedure and club-playbook-document sections.

5. Built to Last: Successful Habits of Visionary Companies - James C. Collins and Jerry I. Porras - HarperBusiness - Collins and Porras are the most widely credited researchers on what allows organizations to outlast any single founder, the capstone theme this chapter closes the book with, echoing Chapter 1's sustainability argument.

6. [Elements of Effective Practice for Mentoring](https://eepm.mentoring.org/) - MENTOR: National Mentoring Partnership - The field's standard framework for mentor training, certification, and peer support, directly supporting this chapter's mentor-training-curriculum and mentor-certification-program sections.

7. [Free Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Templates](https://www.smartsheet.com/standard-operating-procedures) - Smartsheet - Ready-to-adapt SOP templates across formats, directly supporting this chapter's club-playbook-document and standard-operating-procedure sections.

8. [Free Strategic Plan Templates for Nonprofits](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/non-profit-strategic-plan-template) - Smartsheet - Step-by-step guidance and templates for a 3-to-5-year plan, directly supporting this chapter's annual-strategic-plan and multi-year-vision-plan sections.

9. [Board Member Roles and Responsibilities](https://boardsource.org/fundamental-topics-of-nonprofit-board-service/roles-responsibilities/) - BoardSource - Governance guidance relevant to this chapter's leadership-pipeline-development section as a club grows past its founding leader.

10. [Five Levels of Intelligent Textbooks](https://dmccreary.medium.com/five-levels-of-intelligent-textbooks-b81a4c1525a0) - Dan McCreary, Medium - The author's own framework for building lasting, reusable educational infrastructure, echoing this capstone chapter's argument that documentation and systems — not any one leader — are what make a club sustainable.
