# References: AI-Customized Curriculum and Rolling Out New Agents

1. [Algorithmic bias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_bias) - Wikipedia - Explains how automated systems can produce systematically unfair outcomes, the concept behind this chapter's AI-bias-awareness section.

2. [Machine translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_translation) - Wikipedia - Covers how software translates between languages and where it still falls short, the background for this chapter's AI-assisted-translation section.

3. [Mental model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_model) - Wikipedia - Explains how learners build internal representations of how something works, the concept behind this chapter's building-student-mental-models section.

4. Race After Technology: Abolitionist Tools for the New Jim Code - Ruha Benjamin - Polity - Benjamin is widely credited for analyzing how automated systems can encode and amplify bias, the reasoning behind this chapter's AI-bias-awareness section.

5. Mindstorms: Children, Computers, and Powerful Ideas - Seymour Papert - Basic Books - Papert's constructionist theory of how learners build internal mental models through hands-on construction is the direct source for this chapter's building-student-mental-models section.

6. [AI Risk Assessments](https://institute.commonsensemedia.org/risk-assessments) - Common Sense Media, Youth AI Safety Institute - Independent risk ratings for AI tools used with children and in schools, directly supporting this chapter's AI-tool-selection-criteria and AI-agent-vendor-comparison sections.

7. [Getting Started on a Responsible AI Use Policy for Nonprofits](https://candid.org/blogs/how-to-create-responsible-ai-use-policy-for-nonprofits/) - Candid - Practical guidance on scope, data handling, and prohibited uses, supporting this chapter's AI-data-privacy-risk section for a club rolling out a new agent.

8. [What Are AI Agents?](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-agents) - IBM - Explains how an AI agent plans, acts, and reviews its own output, background for this chapter's AI-assisted-lesson-customization and AI-generated-challenge-card sections.

9. [Khanmigo](https://www.khanmigo.ai/) - Khan Academy - Rated low risk by independent evaluators, a real-world model this chapter references for what responsible AI-assisted tutoring and debugging help can look like.

10. [Digital Citizenship Curriculum](https://www.commonsense.org/education/digital-citizenship) - Common Sense Education - Free lessons on evaluating and using AI and digital tools responsibly, supporting this chapter's AI-bias-awareness and AI-tool-selection-criteria sections.
