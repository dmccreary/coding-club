# References: Special Events, Payments, and Term Wrap-Up

1. [Hackathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackathon) - Wikipedia - History and format of time-boxed coding competitions and showcases, relevant to this chapter's competition-event and showcase-event planning sections.

2. [Payment processor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payment_processor) - Wikipedia - Explains how card payments move from a family to an organization's bank account, the mechanism behind this chapter's payment-processing-for-fees section.

3. [Time zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_zone) - Wikipedia - Covers how time zones are defined and observed, relevant to this chapter's time-zone-consideration guidance for multi-site or virtual special events.

4. Event Planning: The Ultimate Guide (3rd Edition) - Judy Allen - Wiley - Allen is widely credited as the standard reference for event-logistics planning — venues, guest speakers, and timing — the framework behind this chapter's field-trip, showcase, and open-house coordination.

5. Fundraising for Social Change (7th Edition) - Kim Klein - Jossey-Bass - Klein is a foundational voice in grassroots nonprofit fundraising, including sliding-scale and scholarship-based access models, the reasoning behind this chapter's scholarship-application-process guidance.

6. [Payment Systems for Organizations](https://squareup.com/us/en/services/nonprofit) - Square - Describes card-processing, invoicing, and payout tools built for nonprofits, directly supporting this chapter's payment-processing-for-fees section.

7. [Use a Family Calendar on Google](https://support.google.com/families/answer/7157782) - Google Families Help - Official instructions for sharing a household calendar, supporting this chapter's calendar-sync-for-families guidance.

8. [10 Best Event Registration Software for Small Nonprofits](https://givebutter.com/blog/best-event-management-software-for-nonprofits) - Givebutter - A feature and pricing comparison of registration platforms, supporting this chapter's registration-software-comparison section.

9. [Set and Manage Your Refund Policy](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/130304/how-to-set-a-refund-policy/) - Eventbrite Help Center - Explains configurable refund windows, relevant background for this chapter's weather-cancellation and makeup-session policies.

10. [27 Fun Ways to Celebrate the End of the School Year](https://www.commonsense.org/education/articles/27-fun-ways-to-celebrate-the-end-of-the-school-year) - Common Sense Education - A menu of in-person and virtual closing activities for recognizing student accomplishments, directly supporting this chapter's end-of-term celebration section.
