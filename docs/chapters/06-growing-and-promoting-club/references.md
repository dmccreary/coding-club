# References: Growing, Branding, and Promoting Your Club

1. [Elevator pitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevator_pitch) - Wikipedia - Explains the origin and structure of a short, persuasive summary, the format this chapter asks club leaders to draft when describing their club to a new partner or family.

2. [Value proposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_proposition) - Wikipedia - Defines how organizations articulate the specific benefit they offer an audience, the concept behind this chapter's value proposition statement exercise.

3. [Franchising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franchising) - Wikipedia - Overview of how an organization replicates its model across new locations under a shared brand, the structure this chapter references when discussing multi-site expansion and franchise-style growth.

4. Forces for Good: The Six Practices of High-Impact Nonprofits (2nd Edition) - Leslie R. Crutchfield and Heather McLeod Grant - Jossey-Bass - Crutchfield and Grant are credited with identifying "working with affiliates" as a defining practice of high-impact nonprofits, the network-and-replication growth model this chapter applies to scaling a club to new sites.

5. The New Rules of Marketing and PR (updated edition) - David Meerman Scott - Wiley - Scott is widely credited with modernizing press release writing for the internet era, showing small organizations how to earn local media coverage directly, the approach this chapter recommends for club promotion.

6. [23 Elevator Pitch Examples to Inspire Your Own](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/elevator-pitch-examples) - HubSpot - A five-step structure and worked examples for building a short, memorable pitch, directly supporting this chapter's elevator-pitch exercise.

7. [How to Write a Press Release](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/press-release-template-ht) - HubSpot - A free template and step-by-step instructions for headline, quote, and boilerplate structure, supporting this chapter's press-release-writing section for local media coverage.

8. [School-Public Library Partnerships](https://www.ala.org/alsc/publications-resources/professional-tools/school-public-library-partnerships) - Association for Library Service to Children (ALA) - A toolkit and case studies for building collaborations between schools and libraries, modeling the school and library partnership outreach this chapter recommends.

9. [Girls Who Code | Start a Club](https://girlswhocode.com/get-involved/start-a-club) - Girls Who Code - Shows how an established club network onboards new leaders and sites, a real-world reference point for this chapter's onboarding and multi-site expansion guidance.

10. [Nonprofit Marketing Guide](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/) - Kivi Leroux Miller - The companion website to Miller's book, offering ongoing articles on low-budget outreach, word-of-mouth referral, and community promotion tactics for small organizations.
