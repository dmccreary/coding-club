# References: Gauging Interest and Holding Your First Meeting

1. [Survey methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survey_methodology) - Wikipedia - Overview of how to design and interpret questionnaires, the underlying method behind the interest survey this chapter uses to gauge demand before founding a club.

2. [Feasibility study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feasibility_study) - Wikipedia - Explains how organizations assess whether a proposed venture is practical before committing resources, the process this chapter applies to a club feasibility assessment.

3. [Word-of-mouth marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Word-of-mouth_marketing) - Wikipedia - Covers how personal recommendations spread awareness of a new offering, the low-cost promotion channel this chapter recommends alongside flyers and social media for a first meeting.

4. The Lean Startup - Eric Ries - Crown Business - Ries popularized building a minimum viable offering and testing it with a small pilot group before scaling, the same "first three students" and pilot-cohort strategy this chapter recommends for a brand-new club.

5. The Nonprofit Marketing Guide - Kivi Leroux Miller - Jossey-Bass - Miller is known for a practical, low-budget approach to nonprofit audience research and word-of-mouth promotion, matching this chapter's guidance on surveying interest and promoting a first meeting.

6. [Start a Club](https://girlswhocode.com/get-involved/start-a-club) - Girls Who Code - A real-world walkthrough of founding a coding club, from application through first session, illustrating the founding-team and feasibility steps this chapter describes.

7. [How to Write Survey Questions That Get Better Responses](https://www.surveymonkey.com/learn/research-and-analysis/writing-survey-questions/) - SurveyMonkey - Ten practical tips for writing clear, neutral survey questions, directly supporting this chapter's interest-survey design guidance.

8. [50+ Flyer Examples, Ideas and Design Tips](https://venngage.com/blog/flyer-examples/) - Venngage - A visual library of event-flyer layouts and design principles, supporting this chapter's advice on designing a flyer to promote a first meeting.

9. [How to Begin Your Code Club Session](https://codeclub.org/2025/10/08/how-to-begin-your-code-club-session) - Code Club, Raspberry Pi Foundation - Concrete icebreaker and rule-setting activities for a group's first session, modeling the first-impression, walk-in experience this chapter designs for new families.

10. [Code Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_Club) - Wikipedia - History and structure of the global Code Club network, a real precedent for the small-and-growing club model this chapter's starting-small strategy follows.
