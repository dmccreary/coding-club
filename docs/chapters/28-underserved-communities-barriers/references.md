# References: Reaching Underserved Communities and Removing Barriers

1. [Digital divide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_divide) - Wikipedia - Explains the gap in technology access between income groups, the background for this chapter's low-income-family-support and free-device-loan-program sections.

2. [Women in computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_computing) - Wikipedia - History of the gender gap in technology fields, the context behind this chapter's recruiting-girls-and-women section.

3. [Universal Design for Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Design_for_Learning) - Wikipedia - Explains the framework for designing instruction accessible to learners with different needs from the start, the model behind this chapter's disability-accommodation section.

4. Brave, Not Perfect: Fear Less, Fail More, and Live Bolder - Reshma Saujani - HarperOne - Saujani, founder of Girls Who Code, is credited with reframing how girls relate to risk and failure in technical fields, the reasoning behind this chapter's recruiting-girls-and-women section.

5. Teaching Every Student in the Digital Age: Universal Design for Learning - David H. Rose and Anne Meyer - ASCD - Rose and Meyer originated Universal Design for Learning at CAST, the framework behind this chapter's disability-accommodation and accessible-session-timing guidance.

6. [Start a Club](https://girlswhocode.com/get-involved/start-a-club) - Girls Who Code - A national model for recruiting girls into coding programs at no cost, directly supporting this chapter's recruiting-girls-and-women and no-fee-club-model sections.

7. [UDL Guidelines](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) - CAST - The official Universal Design for Learning guidelines, available in over 20 languages, directly supporting this chapter's disability-accommodation and language-accessibility sections.

8. [EveryoneOn](https://www.everyoneon.org/) - EveryoneOn - A national nonprofit that matches low-income households with affordable internet and low-cost devices by zip code, a real-world model for this chapter's free-device-loan-program section.

9. [Digital Citizenship Curriculum](https://www.commonsense.org/education/digital-citizenship) - Common Sense Education - Free materials available in multiple languages, supporting this chapter's translated-materials and language-accessibility sections.

10. [Afterschool Alliance](https://afterschoolalliance.org/) - Afterschool Alliance - National data on which families lack access to afterschool programming, supporting this chapter's underserved-community-outreach and transportation-barrier-solution sections.
