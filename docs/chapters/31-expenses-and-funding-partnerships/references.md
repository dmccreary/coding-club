# References: Tracking Expenses and Building Funding Partnerships

1. [Sliding scale fees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sliding_scale_fees) - Wikipedia - Explains variable pricing based on a family's ability to pay, the model behind this chapter's sliding-scale-fee-model section.

2. [Cost–benefit analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost%E2%80%93benefit_analysis) - Wikipedia - Covers how organizations weigh a decision's costs against its benefits, the method behind this chapter's cost-benefit-analysis section.

3. [Depreciation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depreciation) - Wikipedia - Explains how equipment loses value over its useful life, the accounting concept behind this chapter's equipment-depreciation-planning section.

4. Nonprofit Sustainability: Making Strategic Decisions for Financial Viability - Jan Masaoka and Steve Zimmerman - Jossey-Bass - Masaoka and Zimmerman developed the "Matrix Map" for weighing a program's mission impact against its financial viability, the reasoning behind this chapter's cost-benefit-analysis and shared-resource-agreement sections.

5. "Collective Impact" (Stanford Social Innovation Review, Winter 2011) - John Kania and Mark Kramer - FSG - Kania and Kramer originated the "collective impact" framework for cross-sector partnerships around a shared goal, the model behind this chapter's school-district, PTA, library, and robotics-club funding-partnership sections.

6. [Substantiating Charitable Contributions](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/substantiating-charitable-contributions) - Internal Revenue Service - Federal requirements for what a donation receipt must include, directly supporting this chapter's donation-receipt-process section.

7. [Fundraising - Local Leader Kit](https://www.pta.org/local-leader-kit/fundraising) - National PTA - Official guidance on mission-driven fundraising, corporate sponsorship rules, and partnership strategies, directly supporting this chapter's PTA-partnership-funding and sponsor-recognition-plan sections.

8. [A Board's Guide to Fiscal Sponsorship](https://propelnonprofits.org/resources/a-boards-guide-to-fiscal-sponsorship/) - Propel Nonprofits - Explains formal financial structures relevant to this chapter's annual-budget-review and emergency-fund-reserve sections.

9. [Insurance Basics for Nonprofits](https://nonprofitrisk.org/resources/insurance-basics-for-nonprofits/) - Nonprofit Risk Management Center - Background on protecting and valuing organizational equipment, relevant to this chapter's equipment-depreciation-planning section.

10. [Free Equipment Inventory Templates & Spreadsheets](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/equipment-inventory-templates) - Smartsheet - Spreadsheet layouts adaptable into a budget-tracking spreadsheet, directly supporting this chapter's budget-tracking-spreadsheet and expense-reporting-process sections.
