# References: Motivation, Badges, and Growth Mindset Coaching

1. [Motivation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motivation) - Wikipedia - Overview of what drives behavior, including the intrinsic-versus-extrinsic distinction this chapter builds its badge and reward guidance around.

2. [Mindset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindset) - Wikipedia - Explains the fixed-versus-growth mindset distinction, the psychological framework behind this chapter's growth-mindset-coaching section.

3. [Self-determination theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-determination_theory) - Wikipedia - The psychological theory distinguishing intrinsic from extrinsic motivation, the research base behind this chapter's caution about over-relying on badges and leaderboards.

4. Mindset: The New Psychology of Success - Carol S. Dweck - Random House - Dweck originated growth mindset research, distinguishing a growth from a fixed mindset, the framework behind this chapter's growth-mindset-coaching and celebrating-small-wins sections.

5. Drive: The Surprising Truth About What Motivates Us - Daniel H. Pink - Riverhead Books - Pink popularized the research on intrinsic versus extrinsic motivation (autonomy, mastery, and purpose), the reasoning behind this chapter's caution about over-relying on badges and leaderboards.

6. [Open Badges](https://openbadges.org/) - 1EdTech Consortium - The open standard for verifiable digital skill badges, the technical model behind this chapter's badge-design and certificate-of-completion sections.

7. [Using Gamification in Grades 3 to 12 to Ignite Student Learning](https://www.edutopia.org/article/using-gamification-ignite-student-learning/) - Edutopia - Warns against single-winner leaderboards and recommends participation-based rewards, directly supporting this chapter's friendly-competition-design and leaderboard-consideration sections.

8. [A Simple Tool for Fostering Growth Mindset](https://www.edutopia.org/article/simple-tool-fostering-growth-mindset) - Edutopia - A concrete classroom practice for treating mistakes as visible, celebrated learning steps, supporting this chapter's celebrating-small-wins section.

9. [Resources for Teaching Growth Mindset](https://www.edutopia.org/article/growth-mindset-resources/) - Edutopia - A curated set of free posters and lesson materials for growth-mindset coaching language, supporting this chapter's mentor-coaching-language guidance.

10. [Digital Citizenship Curriculum](https://www.commonsense.org/education/digital-citizenship) - Common Sense Education - Free lesson materials that model the progress-tracker-poster and learning-map-display formats this chapter recommends for making growth visible.
