# References: Analyzing Club Data and Choosing the Right Tools

1. [Data visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization) - Wikipedia - Explains how charts and graphs communicate patterns in data, the foundation for this chapter's data-visualization-basics section.

2. [Pivot table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pivot_table) - Wikipedia - Explains how a pivot table summarizes and reorganizes raw data, the spreadsheet feature behind this chapter's pivot-table-basics section.

3. [SMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMS) - Wikipedia - Covers how text messaging works as a communication channel, relevant to this chapter's SMS-notification section for reminders.

4. The Visual Display of Quantitative Information (2nd Edition) - Edward Tufte - Graphics Press - Tufte is the most widely credited authority on clear, honest data visualization, the principles behind this chapter's data-visualization-basics section.

5. Storytelling with Data: A Data Visualization Guide for Business Professionals - Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic - Wiley - Knaflic is credited with translating data-visualization principles into practical, non-technical guidance, the approach behind this chapter's attendance, retention, and enrollment-trend charts.

6. [Create & Use Pivot Tables](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1272900?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) - Google Docs Editors Help - Official step-by-step instructions for summarizing enrollment and attendance data, directly supporting this chapter's pivot-table-basics section.

7. [How to Segment Your Email List](https://mailchimp.com/resources/email-segmentation/) - Mailchimp - Covers automating targeted communication to different audience groups, relevant to this chapter's newsletter-automation and automated-reminder-system sections.

8. [Digital Citizenship Curriculum](https://www.commonsense.org/education/digital-citizenship) - Common Sense Education - Resources for evaluating a tool's data practices before adoption, supporting this chapter's guidance on vetting third-party tools and data-sharing agreements.

9. [Free Google Sheets Inventory Templates](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/google-sheets-inventory-templates) - Smartsheet - Spreadsheet layouts adaptable into a skill-mastery or digital-portfolio tracker, supporting this chapter's digital-portfolio-system section.

10. [FERPA](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa) - U.S. Department of Education, Student Privacy Policy Office - Federal guidance on sharing student records with outside parties, directly relevant to this chapter's data-sharing-agreement section.
