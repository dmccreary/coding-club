Three Years of Club Health Metrics

Four different units share one axis by being indexed to 0-100; compare each line's shape, not its height against the others.

What This Shows

Three lines rise. One does not: mentor retention falls from 70% to 55% in Year 3, in the same year enrollment and the waitlist grow fastest.
This is what an early warning sign looks like -- a single line moving against a set of encouraging ones. Growing enrollment and a growing waitlist are exactly what burns mentors out, so the metrics are not independent: the good news is causing the bad news. Turn off every series except mentor retention to see the trend without the reassurance around it. A club reading only enrollment would call Year 3 its best year.
