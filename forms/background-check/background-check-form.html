Request submitted

Nothing was actually transmitted — this is a demonstration of the confirmation the volunteer would see.

  1. Submitted to the screening service over an encrypted API call, with no manual re-entry.
  2. Service processes the request, typically returning a result within one business day.
  3. Result routed automatically to the requesting activity leader — no dependency on one clerk's availability.
  4. Flagged results go to a human reviewer. The system never auto-approves a flagged record.
  5. On a clear result, you are added to the active-volunteer roster and event notification list.
